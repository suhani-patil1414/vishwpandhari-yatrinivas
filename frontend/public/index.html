About Us

Your Home Away From Home

Vishwpandhari Yatrinivas offers a warm, clean, and spiritually enriching stay for pilgrims and families visiting the holy land of Pandharpur.

We are committed to providing hygienic rooms, wholesome meals, and a peaceful environment so you can focus on your devotion and rest comfortably.

  • ✅ Clean & Hygienic Rooms
  • ✅ Walking Distance from Vitthal Temple
  • ✅ 24/7 Support for Guests
  • ✅ Affordable for Pilgrims & Families
